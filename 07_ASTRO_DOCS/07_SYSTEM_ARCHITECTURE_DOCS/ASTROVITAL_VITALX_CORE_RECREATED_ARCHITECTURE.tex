\begin{abstract}
Long-duration spaceflight exposes human crews to prolonged microgravity, isolation, elevated ambient $CO_2$, and cosmic radiation, inducing cephalad fluid shifts, neuro-ocular syndrome (SANS), bone demineralization, and cardiovascular deconditioning. In deep-space environments (lunar vicinity and interplanetary transit to Mars), real-time ground-based clinical telemedicine is rendered obsolete by relativistic electromagnetic propagation delays (ranging from 2.5 seconds to 24 minutes one-way) and communication blackouts. Conventional machine learning systems relying on probabilistic neural representations introduce unbounded stochastic risks and opaque ``black-box'' failure modes, rendering them inherently uncertifiable under strict aerospace safety guidelines. This document formalizes the architectural specification for \textbf{ASTROVITAL AI : VITALX CORE (RECREATED V1)}. The system implements a partitioned \textbf{80/20 Hybrid Deterministic Architecture}: an adaptive auxiliary layer (20\%) dedicated exclusively to telemetry stabilization and high-frequency motion artifact rejection, bounded by a formal Mathematical Sanity Gate, feeding into an 80\% Supreme First-Principles Biophysical Core that executes deterministic, white-box clinical triage.
\end{abstract}

\section{System Architectural Overview \& Design Philosophy}

The core design philosophy enforces absolute separation of concerns between \textit{untrusted telemetry conditioning} and \textit{life-critical diagnostic determination}. 

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{No Black-Box Clinical Arbiters:} Machine learning models are strictly prohibited from evaluating patient pathology or commanding critical medical interventions.
    \item \textbf{Mathematical Sanity Bounding:} All adaptive filters are governed by physiological conservation laws. In the event of sensor detachment or divergence, the system fails deterministically to historical baseline projections rather than corrupting clinical states.
    \item \textbf{First-Principles Primacy:} Clinical decisions are governed by closed-form hydrodynamic, metabolic, and hemodynamic physical equations.
    \item \textbf{Auditable White-Box Logic:} Every diagnostic transition from Nominal (Green) to Compensatory (Amber) or Critical (Red) is deterministically traceable to exact mathematical threshold violations.
\end{itemize}

\section{Four-Layer Architectural Specification}

The system architecture is structured into four sequentially gated, strictly bounded layers.

\subsection{Layer 0: Ingestion \& Telemetry Vector ($\mathbf{X}_{\text{raw}}$)}
The system ingests a 12-dimensional physiological and habitat telemetry vector $\mathbf{X}(t) \in \mathbb{R}^{12}$:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Cardiovascular Subsystem:} Heart Rate ($\text{HR}$, bpm), Systolic Blood Pressure ($\text{SBP}$, mmHg), Diastolic Blood Pressure ($\text{DBP}$, mmHg), Heart Rate Variability Root Mean Square of Successive Differences ($\text{RMSSD}$, ms).
    \item \textbf{Metabolic \& Respiratory Subsystem:} Peripheral Blood Oxygen Saturation ($\text{SpO}_2$, \%), Core Body Temperature ($T_c$, $^\circ$C), Blood Glucose Concentration ($G$, mg/dL).
    \item \textbf{Neurological \& Circadian Subsystem:} Cumulative Sleep Deficit ($\Delta t_{\text{sleep}}$, hours), Psychomotor Reaction Latency ($\tau_{\text{react}}$, ms).
    \item \textbf{Habitat \& Gravitational Stressors:} Cumulative Microgravity Exposure Duration ($t_{\mu g}$, days), Ambient Spacecraft Cabin Carbon Dioxide Partial Pressure ($P_{\text{ambient}}^{CO_2}$, mmHg), Tri-axial Motion Artifact Intensity ($\|\vec{a}_{\text{accel}}\|$, g).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Layer 1: The 20\% Adaptive Auxiliary Layer (Telemetry Stabilization)}
Operating as an unprivileged auxiliary service, Layer 1 isolates physical signal from microgravity-induced artifacts (sweat droplet dielectric shift, sensor motion decoupling, and skin-electrode impedance variations).

\subsubsection{Innovation 3: Signal Stabilizer \& Sensor Drift Compensator}
Let $S_i(t)$ be the measured sensor stream for channel $i$. The measured signal is modeled as:
\begin{equation}
S_i(t) = S_i^{\text{true}}(t) + \delta_i^{\text{drift}}(t) + \eta_i^{\text{motion}}(\vec{a}, t) + \epsilon(t)
\end{equation}
An adaptive multivariate regression and ensemble noise estimator extracts the stabilized physiological state:
\begin{equation}
\hat{S}_i(t) = \mathcal{F}_{\text{adapt}}\left(S_i(t), \vec{a}_{\text{accel}}(t), \Delta T_c\right)
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{F}_{\text{adapt}}$ maps high-frequency accelerometric noise vectors to baseline physiological waveforms.

\subsubsection{Innovation 6: Biomarker Sensitivity \& Multi-System Stress Weighting}
Layer 1 dynamically ranks the leading divergence indicator across physiological subsystems using a localized sensitivity gradient:
\begin{equation}
\vec{W}_{\text{bio}}(t) = \nabla_{\mathbf{X}} \mathcal{L}_{\text{divergence}}\left(\hat{\mathbf{X}}(t), \mathbf{X}_{\text{baseline}}\right)
\end{equation}
This vector ranks which sub-modality (e.g., autonomic withdrawal vs. respiratory fatigue) exhibits the earliest pre-symptomatic divergence without issuing clinical diagnoses.

\subsection{Layer 2: The Formal Mathematical Sanity \& Boundary Gate}
Before any telemetry packet from Layer 1 enters the supreme diagnostic layer, it must pass through an aerospace-inspired formal bounding box.

\begin{equation}
\mathbf{\Omega}_{\text{bio}} = \left\{ \mathbf{X} \in \mathbb{R}^{12} \; \middle| \;
\begin{aligned}
30 &\le \text{HR} \le 220 \\
50 &\le \text{SpO}_2 \le 100 \\
34.0 &\le T_c \le 42.0 \\
\left| \frac{d(\text{SpO}_2)}{dt} \right| &\le \lambda_{\text{bio\_max}}
\end{aligned}
\right\}
\end{equation}

\subsubsection{Deterministic Fallback Protocol}
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{X}_{\text{sanitized}}(t) = 
\begin{cases}
\hat{\mathbf{X}}(t), & \text{if } \hat{\mathbf{X}}(t) \in \mathbf{\Omega}_{\text{bio}} \land \text{Residual}(\mathcal{F}_{\text{adapt}}) < \gamma_{\text{crit}} \\
\mathbf{X}_{\text{projected}}(t) \cup \{\text{FLAG: HARDWARE\_SENSOR\_DECOUPLED}\}, & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
If sensor decoupling or numerical corruption is detected, the AI output is explicitly discarded. The system projects from verified rolling historical baselines and alerts the astronaut to inspect physical hardware interfaces.

\subsection{Layer 3: The 80\% Supreme Biophysical Core (Innovation 1: White-Box CDSS)}
The diagnostic authority resides completely within closed-form analytical biophysical models and deterministic decision logic.

\subsubsection{Biophysical Model 1: Cephalad Hydrodynamic Fluid Shift \& ICP Index}
In microgravity, the hydrostatic gradient $\rho \cdot g \cdot h$ ceases. Venous blood redistributes towards the cephalad region, elevating central venous pressure and intracranial pressure (ICP), triggering Spaceflight-Associated Neuro-ocular Syndrome (SANS):
\begin{equation}
I_{\text{ICP}}(t) = \alpha_1 \cdot \left(\frac{\text{SBP}(t) + 2\text{DBP}(t)}{3}\right) + \alpha_2 \cdot \log\left(1 + t_{\mu g}\right) + \alpha_3 \cdot P_{\text{ambient}}^{CO_2}(t) - \alpha_4 \cdot \text{RMSSD}(t)
\end{equation}
where $\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3, \alpha_4$ are verified physiological coupling coefficients.

\subsubsection{Biophysical Model 2: Cardiovascular Deconditioning \& Stroke Volume Index}
Cardiac output ($CO$) regulation under autonomic microgravity adaptation is computed as:
\begin{equation}
CO(t) = \text{HR}(t) \times SV(t), \quad SV(t) = SV_0 \cdot \exp\left(-\kappa_{\text{atrophy}} \cdot t_{\mu g}\right) \cdot \left[1 + \beta \cdot \text{ExerciseMetric}(t)\right]
\end{equation}

\subsubsection{Deterministic State Transition Logic (White-Box Triage)}
The system outputs a deterministic clinical classification $C \in \{\text{GREEN}, \text{AMBER}, \text{RED}\}$ governed by transparent, closed-form decision boundaries:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{STATE GREEN (Nominal Physiological Equilibrium):}
    \begin{equation}
    \forall j \in \{1,\dots,12\}, \quad \left| \frac{X_j - \mu_j^{\text{baseline}}}{\sigma_j^{\text{baseline}}} \right| \le 1.5 \quad \land \quad I_{\text{ICP}} < I_{\text{ICP}}^{\text{nominal}}
    \end{equation}
    Operational guidance: Standard mission schedule, baseline nutrition and exercise.

    \item \textbf{STATE AMBER (Compensatory Physiological Stress / Pre-Pathological):}
    \begin{equation}
    1.5 < \left| \frac{X_k - \mu_k^{\text{baseline}}}{\sigma_k^{\text{baseline}}} \right| \le 3.0 \quad \lor \quad I_{\text{ICP}}^{\text{nominal}} \le I_{\text{ICP}} < I_{\text{ICP}}^{\text{crit}} \quad \lor \quad \Delta t_{\text{sleep}} \ge 16.0\,\text{hrs}
    \end{equation}
    Operational guidance: Autonomous Countermeasure Deck Alpha (Cabin lighting spectrum adjustment, 45-minute ergometer resistance, 500 mL targeted electrolyte hydration).

    \item \textbf{STATE RED (Acute Critical Threat / Immediate Decompensation):}
    \begin{equation}
    I_{\text{ICP}} \ge I_{\text{ICP}}^{\text{crit}} \quad \lor \quad \left(\text{SpO}_2 < 88\% \land \text{HR} > 125\,\text{bpm}\right) \quad \lor \quad \text{SBP} \ge 165\,\text{mmHg}
    \end{equation}
    Operational guidance: Critical Protocol Omega (Emergency deployment of Lower Body Negative Pressure [LBNP] suit, immediate 100\% oxygen delivery, immediate suspension of EVA/high-G maneuvers).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Layer 4: Astronaut Autonomous Cockpit Interface}
The human-machine interface satisfies the \textbf{3-Second Non-Physician Rule}:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Single-Glance HUD:} High-contrast tri-color risk banner (Green / Amber / Red).
    \item \textbf{Mathematical Audit Trail:} Direct natural language translation of the violated physical threshold (e.g., \textit{``RED ALERT: Cephalad Fluid Shift Index (2.14) exceeded critical boundary (1.85) driven by elevated cabin CO2 (4.2 mmHg) and 48hr sleep deficit''}).
    \item \textbf{Direct Countermeasure Deck:} Discrete checklist of physical actions required by the crew without medical jargon.
\end{enumerate}
