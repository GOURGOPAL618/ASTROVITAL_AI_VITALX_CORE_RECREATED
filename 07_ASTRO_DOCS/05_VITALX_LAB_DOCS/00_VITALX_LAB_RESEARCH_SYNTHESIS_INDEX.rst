1. Executive Summary & Lab Scope
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The ``05_VITALX_LAB`` research environment serves as the scientific and empirical
testbed for the ASTROVITAL AI architecture. While production code in ``02_AI_CORE``,
``03_DECISION_CORE``, and ``04_ASTRONAUT_COCKPIT`` is strictly frozen and deterministic
under DO-178C DAL C, ``05_VITALX_LAB`` contains the exploratory research workflows,
multi-mission synthesis pipelines, spectral signal quality analyses, biomarker
divergence algorithms, biophysical triage proofs, and the master comparative flight
benchmark.

Research Notebook Structure:

* ``01_multi_mission_data_synthesis.ipynb``: Multi-Mission Ingestion & 12D Tensor Synthesis
* ``02_inno3_signal_cleaning_validation.ipynb``: Inno 3 Adaptive Denoising & SQI Certification
* ``03_inno6_biomarker_divergence.ipynb``: Inno 6 Dynamic Dirichlet Sensitivity Optimization
* ``04_inno1_biophysical_triage_proof.ipynb``: Inno 1 Biophysical Physics & Deterministic Triage Proof
* ``05_master_flight_verification.ipynb``: Master 72-Hour Flight Benchmark (100% vs 85.25%)
